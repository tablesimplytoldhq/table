Privacy Policy — Heists & Scams publishing tool

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The Heists & Scams publishing tool ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Heists & Scams (@heistsandscams) to upload, schedule and manage videos on that channel and to read that channel's own analytics. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it.

Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API (scopes youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl and yt-analytics.readonly). It uses that access only to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing dates, read the status of the channel's own videos, and read the channel's own aggregated analytics.

How data is used

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to manage the Heists & Scams channel. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, kept only for as long as needed to perform these tasks, and never published or shared. The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Viewers of the channel

The app does not collect any data about people who watch the channel. Viewing and commenting on YouTube are governed by YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.
